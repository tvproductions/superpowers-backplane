`t0` is the issue's `updatedAt` immediately before intake. `t1` is its value
after an authorized mutation and must be re-fetched. `L` is the count of
`backplane:*` labels, excluding unrelated labels, which must survive. No
mutation means `updatedAt=t0`; a successful issue mutation means a newly
observed `t1>t0`, even when the label stays the same (for example, a blocker
comment). This notation describes expected outcomes, not observed timestamps.
